# City council: winter road maintenance schedule

The city council informs residents that the winter road maintenance season begins on 1 December. Snow clearing on main roads and public transport routes starts as soon as snowfall exceeds 2 cm. Residential streets are cleared within 24 hours after the main roads.

Residents are asked not to park on streets marked with temporary "Snow clearing" signs between 22:00 and 06:00. Vehicles that block the machinery may be towed.

Sand and salt bins are installed at 320 public stops and pedestrian crossings. To report an uncleared street or an empty bin, call the city hotline 1580 or use the city's mobile app.

The full schedule and a map of priority routes are published on the city council website.
