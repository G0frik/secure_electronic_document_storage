# Project Birch — acquisition proposal for the board

Summary: we propose acquiring a regional cold-chain carrier ("Target B") before our competitors finish their own due diligence. The deal has not been announced and must not be discussed outside the board and the deal team.

Valuation: our model values Target B at 6.5–7.2 times next year's EBITDA. We would open with an offer at the low end and accept up to 7.0 times if the founders stay for two years.

Rationale: Target B owns refrigerated warehouses in three cities where we have no presence. Combined, we expect cost savings of about 11% in the first full year, mostly from shared routing and fleet maintenance.

Risks: the antitrust review could take six months; two key customers have change-of-control clauses.

Next step: board approval to submit a non-binding offer by 30 November.
