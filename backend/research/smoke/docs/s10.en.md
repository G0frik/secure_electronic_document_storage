# Occupational health: consultation summary

Reason for referral: the employee asked for an adjustment of working hours after repeated sick leave this autumn.

History: over the past four months the employee reported persistent fatigue, low mood, poor sleep and difficulty concentrating. The family doctor diagnosed a moderate depressive episode in September and prescribed sertraline 50 mg daily, increased to 100 mg in October. The employee also attends weekly psychotherapy sessions.

Assessment: the symptoms are improving but not yet stable. Night shifts and on-call duty are likely to worsen sleep and delay recovery.

Recommendation: day shifts only for three months, no on-call duty, and permission to leave early on therapy days. Review in February.

The diagnosis and treatment details are for occupational health use only; the manager receives the recommendation without medical details.
