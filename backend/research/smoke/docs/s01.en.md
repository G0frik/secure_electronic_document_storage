# Northwind Logistics opens a new distribution hub in Lviv

LVIV, 4 November 2026 — Northwind Logistics today announced the opening of its new regional distribution hub in the Sykhiv industrial zone. The 18,000 square metre facility will serve retail customers across western Ukraine and cut average delivery times in the region from three days to one.

The hub employs 140 people, and the company plans to hire 60 more by spring. Applications are open on the company's careers page.

"Western Ukraine is one of our fastest-growing markets," said the company's chief operating officer. "This hub lets us bring parcels closer to the people who ordered them."

The facility runs on rooftop solar panels that cover about a third of its electricity needs. Visitors are welcome at an open day on 15 November from 10:00 to 16:00.
