# Notes: annual compensation review, engineering

The compensation committee met to agree the engineering salary adjustments for next year.

The overall budget for raises is 9% of the current engineering payroll. Market data shows our senior engineers are paid about 12% below the median for the Lviv and Kyiv markets, so most of the budget goes to the senior band. Mid-level engineers receive a standard 6% increase; juniors receive 5%.

The two team leads who were promoted this year move to the new lead band, which starts 18% above the senior band. Retention bonuses equal to two months' salary are approved for the three engineers working on the platform migration, paid in two parts.

The committee rejected a proposal to freeze salaries in the support team; their raises follow the standard grid.

Individual letters go out on 10 December. Managers must not share the band figures with their teams before then.
